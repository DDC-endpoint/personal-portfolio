# V3: price research (October 2026)

Damian asked for a research pass on the `/services` prices at the Phase 4 gate (2026-10-27) and chose to adopt every suggestion below. The prices live in `lib/services.js`; the "Prices updated October 2026." line under the pricing note dates them. Re-run this research before the next change.

**How it was done:** one research agent, web searches only. 17 Sri Lankan price lists and guides (agencies, freelancers, SEO studios), 9 international sources (Fiverr and Upwork rate guides, freelance cost guides). Most are dated 2026; several vendor pages are undated, and vendor pages have commercial bias. Exchange rate used: about LKR 330 to the dollar (early October 2026).

## Result

| Service | Before | LKR market (typical) | Now (from) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web design & development (landing page) | LKR 18,000 / $150 | 30–40k; budget freelancers 15–30k | **LKR 30,000 / $300** |
| Business & portfolio sites (up to ~5 pages) | LKR 40,000 / $500 | 60–80k; agencies 80–95k+ | **LKR 60,000 / $600** |
| Booking & scheduling sites | LKR 65,000 / $800 | 120–150k; one studio charges 65k without a payment gateway and 85k with one | **LKR 120,000 / $1,200** |
| Redesigns & speed fixes | LKR 25,000 / $200 | no published prices found | **LKR 30,000 / $300** |
| SEO & AI visibility audit | LKR 50,000 / $299 | 35–45k; some agencies audit free | **LKR 40,000 / $400** |
| SEO & GEO implementation | LKR 65,000 / $400 | about 75k | **LKR 75,000 / $750** |
| Ongoing SEO & GEO retainer | LKR 48,000 / $225 a month | 45–50k a month | **LKR 45,000 / $400 a month** |
| Larger, fully custom builds | from LKR 150,000 | 200–300k | **from LKR 250,000** |

## Why

- **The booking site was the most underpriced service:** it included a payment gateway for less than local studios charge without one.
- **The USD prices followed no rule.** They implied anywhere from 80 to 213 rupees per dollar. They now follow one: USD ≈ LKR ÷ 100. That's about 3.3 times the LKR price at the market rate, which is normal for international clients and still at the low end of global freelance rates (projects from $300–800; South Asian developers at $15–45 an hour).
- **Credibility:** every price stays below agency rates and at or near the freelance median, which suits four public projects and no testimonials yet. With two or three testimonials, the next step is toward the freelance midpoint (a business site at about LKR 80,000).
- **Hosting:** many local packages include the first year of hosting and a domain (worth about LKR 5,000–20,000 a year). These prices bill both at cost, so they compare slightly better than they look.
- **Not found:** a USD price for a freelance booking site, a USD SEO retainer from a South Asian freelancer, and any LKR price for a redesign. Those three prices are estimates.
